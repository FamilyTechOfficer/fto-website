Secure Small Business Networks

Your business runs on information. Your network should protect it.

Customer records, payments, passwords, and files all travel over your network every day. In most small businesses, everything shares one network: staff computers, the card reader, the cameras, and every customer on the Wi-Fi. That's convenient, but it isn't secure. The fix is separate networks for your business, your guests, and your devices, set up right and handed over to you.

If anything goes wrong, call me. No contract, no monthly fee.

What You're Protected From

What a secure network
protects you from.

Most attacks on small businesses aren't aimed at you specifically. They're automated, looking for anything easy. A secure network makes you a harder target and limits the damage if something does get through.

  • Viruses and ransomware. If one computer gets infected, it can't spread to the rest of your office.
  • Hacked cameras and gadgets. Cameras and smart devices are common targets. On their own network, a hacked device can't reach your computers or files.
  • Strangers on your Wi-Fi. Customers and visitors get internet access, and nothing else.
  • Your payment and customer information. The devices that handle money and records are kept separate from everything else.

No network can stop every threat. Backups, updates, and good passwords still matter. But separating your network is one of the simplest, most effective steps a small business can take.
