A Secure Network for Your Small Business

Built right once. Yours to keep. No monthly contract.

Your network carries your customer records, your payments, and your passwords. Most small businesses end up with one of two options, and neither one is a great fit. There's a better middle ground.

Your three options

The shared router

The box from your internet provider. Staff, guests, and devices all on one network.
  • No monthly fee
  • Guests on the same network as your computers
  • One virus can spread to every device
  • Nobody to call when it breaks
Cheap, but wide open.

The IT contract

A managed IT company with a monthly fee, often locked in for years.
  • Secure setup
  • You pay every month, forever
  • They often hold the passwords
  • Hard to leave if you're unhappy
Secure, but you rent it.

The FTO way

A business-grade network, designed and installed once, then handed to you.
  • Staff, guests, and devices kept apart
  • No monthly fees, no contract
  • You own the equipment and passwords
  • Same person to call, every time
Secure, and it's yours.
